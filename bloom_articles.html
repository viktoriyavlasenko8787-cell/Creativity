Little words, big difference

In English, tiny words like a, an and the appear in almost every sentence. And sometimes there is no article at all. We call that the zero article and write it like this: Ø. This issue is your guide. First read the study cards, then practise with stories, chats and diaries from teen life.

Bloom featureStudy card 1
Study card: a / an

A and an: the “any one” words

A and an mean “one, any one, not a special one”. We use them only with singular countable nouns: a phone, an idea, a friend. Never with plurals (a books) and never with uncountable nouns (a music, a homework).

a

+ consonant sound

a book, a hoodie, a selfie, a university /juː/, a European /jʊə/, a one-day trip /w/

an

+ vowel sound

an apple, an idea, an umbrella, an hour (silent h), an honest girl, an e-mail

Listen, don't look! It's the first sound that matters, not the first letter.

When do we use a / an?

1

First mention

We talk about something for the first time.

I saw a cute dog in the park.
2

One of many, any one

It's not important which one.

Can I borrow a pen? I need a new phone case.
3

Jobs

We say what someone does.

My mum is a nurse. I want to be an artist.
4

Describing

We say what someone or something is like.

She's a kind girl. It's an amazing song!
5

What a…!

Exclamations with a singular noun.

What a beautiful dress! What an awful day!
6

How often, how much

“Per” in everyday English.

twice a week, three times a day, €5 an hour
7

Set phrases

Learn them as one piece.

have a shower, have a good time, a lot of, a few, in a hurry
Bloom featureStudy card 2
Study card: the

The: the one we both know

The means “this exact one, you know which!”. It works with all kinds of nouns: singular (the phone), plural (the phones) and uncountable (the music).

The story trick: a first, then theI've got a cat and a hamster. The cat is lazy, but the hamster runs all night!

When do we use the?

1

Second mention

We already talked about it.

I bought a hoodie. The hoodie is pink.
2

Only one in the world

There is just one.

the sun, the moon, the sky, the internet, the world
3

We both know which

It's clear from the situation.

Close the door, please. Where's the teacher?
4

A phrase tells us which

Words after the noun make it specific.

the girl in blue jeans, the capital of Spain, the song you sent me
5

Superlatives and order

Number one, first, last…

the best, the funniest, the first, the last, the same, the only
6

Musical instruments

With play.

play the guitar, play the piano, play the drums
7

Geography

Rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, and countries with States, Kingdom or a plural name.

the Dnipro, the Black Sea, the Atlantic, the Carpathians, the USA, the UK, the Netherlands
8

Set phrases

Learn them as one piece.

in the morning, go to the cinema, listen to the radio, the Smiths (the Smith family)
Bloom featureStudy card 3
Study card: zero article

Ø: when nothing is perfect

Sometimes the best article is no article at all! We call it the zero article Ø. We use it most often when we talk about things in general.

General or specific?Ø Pizza is my favourite food. (all pizza)
The pizza at Kate's party was cold. (that pizza)

When do we use no article?

1

Plurals in general

All of them, not specific ones.

Ø Cats are clever. I love Ø sneakers.
2

Uncountables in general

Music, homework, water, money…

Ø Music helps me relax. Ø Homework is boring!
3

Names

People, cities, most countries, streets, single mountains and lakes.

Ø Anna, Ø Lviv, Ø Ukraine, Ø Oxford Street, Ø Mount Everest, Ø Lake Svitiaz
4

Meals

Breakfast, lunch, dinner.

have Ø breakfast, after Ø lunch, before Ø dinner
5

Sports and games

With play.

play Ø football, play Ø volleyball, play Ø chess
6

School subjects and languages

What you study and speak.

I'm good at Ø maths. She speaks Ø English.
7

Days, months, holidays

Time words.

on Ø Monday, in Ø July, at Ø Christmas
8

Places and their main use

We go there for its usual purpose.

go to Ø school, at Ø home, go to Ø bed, in Ø hospital, at Ø work
9

By + transport

How you travel.

by Ø bus, by Ø bike, by Ø train (but: on foot)
10

Set phrases

Learn them as one piece.

at Ø night, watch Ø TV, Ø next week, Ø last year

Study card: tricky pairs

Same word, different article

One small change and the meaning changes too. Compare the green side with the purple side.

I go to Ø school.I'm a student there.
vs
Mum went to the school.She visited the building.
I play Ø tennis.a sport
vs
I play the violin.an instrument
I love Ø music.music in general
vs
The music at the party was too loud.that music
We have Ø lunch at one.a meal
vs
We had a lovely lunch.adjective + meal
I'm in Ø bed.sleeping or resting
vs
My cat is on the bed.the piece of furniture
I live in Ø Ukraine.most countries
vs
She lives in the UK.Kingdom, States, plural names
I watch Ø TV.set phrase
vs
I listen to the radio.set phrase

The 3-second test

Is it a name, a meal, a sport, a subject, a language or a day?→ Ø
Do we both know which one? Is it the only one, or number one?→ the
Is it one singular thing, any one, or the first time we mention it?→ a / an
Is it plural or uncountable, and we mean it in general?→ Ø
