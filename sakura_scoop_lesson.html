The rules exposed

Two tenses, two personalities. One loves routines, the other lives in the moment.

☀️ Present SimpleThe routine queen: habits & facts 🎬 Present ContinuousThe drama star: happening now
When do we use it?
  • habits and routines
  • facts and things that are always true
  • likes, feelings, opinions
  • actions happening right now
  • temporary situations (this week, these days)

😱 Scandal: the stative verbs

Some verbs describe feelings and thoughts, not actions. They almost never take -ing:

love, like, hate, want, need, know, understand, believe, prefer

I am loving sushi. ✗   I love sushi. ✓

💄 Spelling secrets

he/she/it + s: watch → watches, study → studies, go → goes

-ing: make → making, swim → swimming, lie → lying

Frequency words go before the main verb but after be: I always bow. She is never late.
